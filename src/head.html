How to play
  • 1 year = 1 minute. You get a peace period to build up before war starts. Borders are closed, but you can claim neutral land and cities.
  • Build tab: houses (more citizens), farms, lumber mills, mines on resource deposits, oil wells, steel mills, arms factories and walls (drag to paint them).
  • Citizens work your buildings, pay taxes and become manpower. Keep them fed, housed and happy, or they protest and riot. Bombs and battles near cities scare them.
  • Units cost money, resources and manpower. Infantry need guns. Tanks and planes need steel and burn petrol.
  • Market tab: buy and sell on the internal market. Prices move with supply and demand.
  • World tab: trade with neutral countries, hire foreign manpower and sign treaties. Allies send troops. The enemy is courting them too.
  • Drag to pan, pinch to zoom. Tap a unit, then tap the map to move. Hold & drag to box-select.
  • Conquer city by city. Each city controls its province. Move ground troops into a city with no defenders left and hold it until the ring fills. Capitals take longest. Hurt units auto-retreat to heal. Take the enemy capital to win.
  • The enemy plays by your rules: same economy, same map (mirrored), and it only knows what its scouts have seen.
