# RealWar (DOTFRONT)

A mobile-first real-time war and nation-building game in a single HTML file.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no dependencies, works offline.

## What's in the game
- Real-time battles with rifle squads, tanks, howitzers, anti-air, fighters and bombers. Bullets, shells and bombs travel visibly, and destroyed units leave wrecks and craters.
- City-by-city conquest. Each city controls a province; take it by holding the city with no defenders left.
- Economy: food, wood, coal, iron, petrol, steel, guns, consumer goods and gold. You build farms, mines, oil wells, steel mills, arms factories, goods factories, housing and walls.
- **Builders.** Placing a building pays for it and starts a construction site. Builder units (yellow hard hats) carry materials from your nearest city, then build it. The site's bar shows materials delivered (amber) and construction done (green). Idle builders repair damaged buildings and walls. Recruit them at any city or with **+ Builder** in the Build tab.
- **Making money.** Besides taxes, Goods Factories make consumer goods, Market Halls sell them to your citizens for cash, and Banks turn gold into steady income. Citizens with goods are happier.
- **Missiles and nukes.** Build a Missile Silo, then build cruise missiles or nuclear warheads in its Arsenal and tap **STRIKE** to aim. Missiles wreck troops, buildings and walls; nearby enemy anti-air can shoot them down. A nuke wipes out a city's people and everything in its blast, leaves fallout for two minutes, and makes every nation in the world hate you. You can hold at most 2 nukes, and strikes are only possible in wartime.
- Detailed towns and buildings with people working in them: miners with carts, lumberjacks, farmhands, shoppers. Citizens walk the streets and can be killed by fighting, bombs and strikes.
- Citizens who work, pay taxes, become manpower, and react to war with happiness, protests, riots, refugees and voices.
- An internal market plus 6 neutral nations to trade with, hire from and sign treaties with. They are drawn across the sea around the war map: zoom out to see them, and tap one to open its page.
- A fair AI: same economy, mirrored map, and a human-like action rate. Difficulty only changes how fast and well it thinks.

## Controls
Works on phones and PCs; press **?** in game (or **Controls** in the menu) for the full list.
- **PC:** left-click to select, left-drag to box-select (Shift adds), double-click to select a whole division or unit type. Right-click to move or attack, right/middle-drag to pan, and scroll to zoom. On the keyboard: WASD or arrows to pan, Ctrl+A for the whole army, Ctrl+1–9 to save a group and 1–9 to recall it (twice to jump), F form division, X hold, R retreat, T refuel, Q/B/M/G/P for tabs, C capital, Z speed, Space pause. Edge scrolling can be switched off in the pause menu.
- **Phone:** tap to select, then tap the map to move. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, hold and drag to box-select.
- **Strikes:** tap **STRIKE** (or the silo's Launch button), tap the target, then Launch. On PC, Enter launches and Esc cancels.
- **Interface size:** Small, Normal or Large, in the menu and the pause menu.

## Training
**Training** in the menu is a 13-step guided mission against a passive enemy. You move the map, select and move troops, capture a city, build a farm and watch builders finish it, build an iron mine, recruit, form a division, then use the market, people and world tabs before going to war. Each step waits until you actually do it, highlights the button to press, and has **Show me** / **Skip**.

## Online multiplayer
Tap **PLAY ONLINE**, enter a name and tap **FIND MATCH**. The first two players in the queue are paired.

- **No peace time.** Matches start right away, and you can build, trade and invade whenever you like.
- **Same rules for both players.** The match runs on the server and phones only display it and send orders, so nobody can cheat or fall out of sync.
- **Mirrored view.** Each player sees themselves as blue with their base at the bottom.
- **Reconnecting.** If your connection drops, the game reconnects automatically. A player who stays away for 60 seconds forfeits. After 40 minutes, whoever holds more land wins.

The server is a Cloudflare Worker with Durable Objects (free plan). It lives in `server/`:
| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `server/src/worker.js` | Cloudflare entry point: the lobby and one Durable Object per match |
| `server/src/core.js` | Matchmaking queue and match rooms (platform-neutral) |
| `server/src/sim.js` | The game rules, generated by `build.sh` from `src/`. Do not edit by hand. |
| `wrangler.toml` (repo root, mirrored in `server/`) | Worker name (`realwar`) and Durable Object setup |

**Deploying the server:** the Worker is connected to this repo with Cloudflare Workers Builds, so every merge to `main` redeploys it. The game connects to `realwar.zerapagamentos.workers.dev` by default; another address can be entered in **PLAY ONLINE → Server address**.

**Testing locally:** `node test/mpserver.mjs` runs the same lobby and rooms on `ws://localhost:8787`. `test/online.mjs` plays a full two-browser match against it.

## Project layout
| Path | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The built game. This is the only file needed to play or host. |
| `src/` | Source modules, joined in order by `build.sh` |
| `build.sh` | Rebuilds `index.html` and `server/src/sim.js` from `src/` |
| `server/` | Multiplayer server (Cloudflare Worker) |
| `test/` | Headless Playwright checks (simulations, UI flows, screenshots) |
| `PLAN.md` | Design notes |

Source modules: `builders.js` (construction sites and builder jobs), `strikes.js` (silos, missiles, nukes, fallout), `world.js` (neutral nations around the map, town and building art, people, strike effects), `pc.js` (keyboard, edge scrolling, control groups), `tutorial.js` (training mission), `head.html` (styles and markup), `data.js` (constants, icons, flags, portraits), `map.js` (map generation and pathfinding), `game.js` (units, combat, conquest, simulation step), `econ.js` (economy, diplomacy, citizens), `ai.js`, `commands.js` (every player action, shared with the server), `render.js`, `art.js` (unit art and effects), `mp.js` (online client), `ui.js`, and `server-shim.js` (server build only).

## Editing
Change files in `src/`, then run `./build.sh` and commit `index.html` and `server/src/sim.js` together. Never edit those two files by hand, because the next build overwrites them.

The tests need Node and Playwright with Chromium; the browser path is set at the top of each test file.
